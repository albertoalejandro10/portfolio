## On your rate: $27.78/hr

- The number itself is fine as a *floor* — but it reads as odd/arbitrary to clients. It looks like you reverse-engineered it to net $25 after Upwork's 10% fee ($27.78 × 0.9 ≈ $25.00). Clients don't know that; they just see a strange number. **Round it: $30/hr** (nets you $27). Round numbers read as confidence; $27.78 reads as spreadsheet.
- For your actual profile — 4+ yrs, full-stack, production launches, payments, AWS, testimonials — $30 is still on the **low end**. Comparable full-stack Nuxt/Vue devs on Upwork commonly list $35–60/hr. A reasonable path: list **$30–35 now**, raise to $40+ after your next 2–3 five-star Upwork contracts.
- Whatever you choose, **update the FAQ in `content/en/index.yml` (and `es`) — it still says $15/hr**, which contradicts your Upwork rate and anchors you down if a client finds your portfolio.
